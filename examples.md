The city with the most product returns is Lucknow, which recorded a total of 7 returns during the analyzed period. This suggests that Lucknow had a higher incidence of customers returning items compared to other cities in the dataset.

Regarding the return policies applicable:

- **General Returns:** Customers can return unused and unopened products within 30 days of purchase, provided they have a valid receipt or order ID. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received.
  
- **Groceries & Perishables:** These items are generally non-returnable once delivered, except if they are damaged or expired. Such issues must be reported within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for a full refund or replacement.
  
- **Clothing & Apparel:** Clothing can be returned within 30 days if tags are still attached and the item is unworn. However, innerwear, swimwear, and customized or altered garments are final sale and cannot be returned.

6. How should we proceed if we get a damaged or incorrect item
Business Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, you should report the issue within 48 hours via the "My Orders > Report an Issue" section. RetailIQ will then arrange a free pickup of the item and either send a replacement or issue a full refund if a replacement is not available. 

For returns of damaged or incorrect items, a prepaid return shipping label is provided, so customers do not bear the return shipping cost. This process is designed to be convenient and cost-free for the customer in these cases.

The data shows that all returns related to damaged or incorrect items are actually due to damaged products, with no reported cases of incorrect items. This suggests that damage is the main problem to address. Therefore, it is important to focus on improving packaging, handling, and quality control to reduce damage occurrences.

In summary, the recommended procedure is:
- Report the damaged item within 48 hours through the app or website.
- RetailIQ arranges free pickup and replacement or refund.
- Use prepaid return labels for damaged/incorrect items.
- Internally, prioritize reducing damage through better packaging and handling.

This approach ensures a smooth customer experience and helps reduce the root cause of returns.
