RetailIQ Store — Discount & Promotions Policy 
1. Seasonal Sales 
RetailIQ runs three major seasonal sales each year: the New Year Sale (January), the 
Monsoon Sale (July), and the Festive Sale (October-November). Discounts during these 
events range from 10% to 60% depending on category and are pre-announced on the 
homepage at least 5 days in advance. 
2. Loyalty Program Discounts 
Members of the RetailIQ Plus loyalty program receive an additional flat 5% discount on 
top of any ongoing sale, capped at INR 2,000 per order. Loyalty points earned on a 
purchase equal 2% of the order value and can be redeemed on future orders (1 point = 
INR 1). 
3. Bulk / Wholesale Discounts 
Orders containing 10 or more units of the same product qualify for a bulk discount of 
8%, applied automatically at checkout. This does not stack with seasonal sale 
discounts — the higher of the two applies. 
4. Coupon Codes 
Coupon codes are single-use per account unless stated otherwise, cannot be 
combined with other coupon codes, and typically expire 30 days after issue. Expired 
coupons cannot be reactivated or extended. 
5. Employee & Partner Discounts 
Employees receive a standing 15% discount on all categories except electronics, which 
is capped at 8%. 
This discount is for personal use only and is not transferable. 
6. Price Adjustment Policy 
If a purchased item drops in price within 7 days of purchase (excluding flash sales), 
customers can request the price difference be refunded as loyalty points by contacting 
support.
