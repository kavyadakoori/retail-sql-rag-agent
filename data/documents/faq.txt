RetailIQ Store — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does standard delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days for metro cities and 5-8 business days for non-metro areas.
Express delivery (available in select cities) delivers within 24 hours for an additional fee of INR 149.

Q: Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
A: Yes, COD is available for orders under INR 10,000. A convenience fee of INR 25 applies to COD orders.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Order tracking is available under "My Orders" once the item is shipped, or via the tracking link sent
by email/SMS. Tracking typically updates within 6-12 hours of a status change.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: RetailIQ accepts credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, RetailIQ Wallet, and COD (subject to eligibility).

Q: Is international shipping available?
A: Currently, RetailIQ ships only within India. International shipping is on the roadmap but not yet live.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: Support is available via in-app chat (9 AM - 9 PM daily) or email at support@retailiq.example.com,
with a typical first response time of under 2 hours during business hours.

Q: What should I do if I receive a damaged or wrong item?
A: Report it within 48 hours via "My Orders > Report an Issue." RetailIQ will arrange a free pickup and
send a replacement, or issue a full refund if a replacement isn't available.
