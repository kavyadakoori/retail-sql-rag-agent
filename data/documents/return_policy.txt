RetailIQ Store — Return & Refund Policy

1)	General Returns
a)	Customers may return unused, unopened products within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt or order ID.
b)	Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the returned item being received.

2)	Electronics
a)	Electronics (phones, laptops, headphones, appliances) have a shorter return window of 15 days from delivery.
b)	The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories included. A 10% restocking fee applies if the box has been opened and the item shows signs of use beyond basic testing.

3)	Groceries & Perishables
Perishable and grocery items are non-returnable once delivered, except in cases of damaged or expired products, which must be reported within 48 hours of delivery for a full refund or replacement.

4)	Clothing & Apparel
Clothing can be returned within 30 days if tags are still attached and the item is unworn. Innerwear, swimwear, and customized/altered garments are final sale and cannot be returned.

5)	Exchanges
a)	Exchanges for a different size or color follow the same window as returns for that product category.
b)	Exchanges are subject to stock availability at the time of request.

6)	How to Initiate a Return
Customers can initiate a return through the "My Orders" section of the app or website, or by contacting customer support with their order ID. A prepaid return shipping label is provided for defective or incorrect items; for other returns, a shipping fee of INR 99 is deducted from the refund.
